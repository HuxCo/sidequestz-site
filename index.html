What to write about

  • A name or handle the filter refused that it shouldn't have
  • An account you can't get into
  • A question about what the app holds about you — the answer is short
  • A quest that shouldn't be in there
  • Anything the app itself got wrong

Deleting your account, or all of it

  • One account: Settings → Danger zone → Delete this account. The profile, its XP and its whole log go with it, and that can't be undone. Other accounts on the same phone are untouched.
  • Everything: delete the app. It all lives on the phone, so nothing is left behind on a server — there isn't one.

There's no copy anywhere to restore from afterwards, so it really is gone.

Common questions

Why won't it let me use that name?

Display names and handles run through a filter, because they're the one thing other people on the phone see. It's deliberately blunt, and it does sometimes refuse a word that was perfectly innocent — a surname, a place. If that happens, write to us with the word and it gets fixed.

Can my friends join from their own phones?

Not yet. Everything lives on the device, so the people you add have to be accounts on the same phone — which is what makes passing it round a group work. Nothing you do is sent anywhere.

Where did my progress go?

It was on the phone and nowhere else. Deleting the app, or deleting the account in Settings, takes it with it, and there's no server holding a copy to restore from. That's the trade for collecting nothing.

Is there a feed? Can I post the videos?

No, by design. The app deals you something to do and keeps score — what you film is yours, and it stays in your camera roll. Quest pages link out to TikTok and YouTube if you want to see how other people have done one.

Two of us use one phone.

That's the design. Each account has its own four-digit PIN, its own crew, its own XP and its own log, and switching accounts switches all of it. Nobody's progress is shared.

Somebody filmed me doing a quest.

Whatever they shot is on their phone, not ours — the app never uploads anything and never had a copy. Ask them to delete it. The Quest Code is clear that anyone recognisable has to be fine with being filmed, and that the first no is the answer.
