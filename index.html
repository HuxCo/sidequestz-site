Write to us

What to write about

  • Something against the rules that's still up
  • A post of yours taken down that shouldn't have been
  • Somebody who filmed you, or posted you, without asking
  • An account you can't get into
  • A word the filter refused that it shouldn't have
  • Anything the app itself got wrong

If it's urgent — somebody in danger — say so in the subject and it jumps the queue.

Reporting something inside the app

Faster than mail, and it goes to the same person. On any clip or comment, tap the ··· and choose Report.

  1. It's hidden from you the moment you report it.
  2. Once enough different people report the same thing, it's hidden from everybody until it's been looked at.
  3. A person reads every report, normally inside a day or two.
  4. What comes down comes down for everyone, and whoever posted it is told which rule it broke.

Nobody is ever told who reported them. You can follow your own reports in Settings, under Your reports.

If your post was taken down

You'll get a notice in the app saying which rule it broke, with a link to argue with it. Or write to sidequestzapp@gmail.com with "appeal" in the subject. A person re-reads it, and says yes or no either way.

Deleting your account, or just one clip

  • One clip: the ··· on your own clip, then Delete.
  • Everything: Settings → Danger zone → Delete this account. The profile, its clips, its XP and its whole log go with it, and that can't be undone. Other accounts on the same phone are untouched.
  • All of it at once: delete the app. Everything lives on the phone, so nothing is left behind on a server — there isn't one.

Want to know what's held about you first? Ask, and you'll get a straight answer.

Common questions

Where did my clip go?

Clips are swept on a timer you choose in Settings, under Videos. It starts at 90 days. Whatever that says, only your newest 60 unkept clips are held — mark one as kept and it stays until you delete it yourself.

Who can see what I post?

New accounts are friends-only: somebody has to send you a request and have you accept it before your clips open up to them. Settings → Videos → Who can watch them switches between that and anyone who looks up your handle. Changing it applies to everything you've already posted, not just the next one.

Why won't it let me use that word?

Names, handles, captions and comments run through a filter. It's deliberately blunt, and it does sometimes refuse a word that was perfectly innocent — a surname, a place. If that happens, write to us with the word and it gets fixed.

There's no sound.

Clips play through the phone's silent switch on purpose, because you chose to watch them. Each clip has its own mute button in the feed if you'd rather it didn't.

Two of us use one phone.

That's the design. Each account has its own four-digit PIN, its own crew, its own XP and its own clips, and switching accounts switches all of it. Nobody's progress is shared.

Somebody filmed me and I want it gone.

Write to us. You don't need an account to ask, and you don't have to know who posted it — tell us where and roughly when, and we'll find it.
