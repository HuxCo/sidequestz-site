Privacy

Updated 10 October 2026

The short version: nothing you do in SideQuestz leaves your phone. There's no server to send it to, no account held by us, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and nothing sold to anybody. We couldn't read your clips if we wanted to.

What's stored, and where

All of it sits in the app's own storage on your device, and nowhere else:

  • Your profile — the name and handle you chose, a four-digit PIN, an avatar if you picked one
  • Your progress — XP, levels, the log of quests you've run
  • Your crew, and friend requests between accounts on the same phone
  • Clips you post, as video files, plus their captions, likes and comments
  • Reports you've filed, and notices about your own posts

Deleting an account deletes its share of that. Deleting the app takes the lot.

Your camera, microphone and photos

The app never opens your camera or microphone — it has no code that can. A clip is a video you already have, handed over through Apple's own picker, which runs outside the app: it shows you your library, you choose one item, and only that item is copied in. The app can't see the rest of your photos, and it never asks for permission to, because it doesn't need it.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage statistics
  • No advertising identifier, no ad networks, no third-party code of any kind
  • No location, no contacts, no email address, no phone number
  • No account on anybody's server, because there is no server

How long clips are kept

You choose, in Settings under Videos. It starts at 90 days, and only your newest 60 unkept clips are held whatever the window says. Mark a clip as kept and it stays until you delete it. Deleting a clip deletes the video file with it.

Children

SideQuestz is for people aged 13 and over. Nothing is collected from anybody, of any age, because nothing is collected at all.

If this ever changes

If clips ever sync between phones — the only change that would move anything off your device — this page changes first, the app says so plainly, and you get asked before anything of yours is sent anywhere.

Asking about any of this

Write to sidequestzapp@gmail.com. One person reads it, and you'll get a straight answer.
