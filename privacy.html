Privacy

Updated 19 October 2026

The short version: nothing you do in SideQuestz leaves your phone. There's no server to send it to, no account held by us, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and nothing sold to anybody. The app has no networking code in it at all.

What's stored, and where

All of it sits in the app's own storage on your device, and nowhere else:

  • Your profile — the name and handle you chose, a four-digit PIN, an avatar if you picked one
  • Your progress — XP, levels, and the log of quests you've run
  • Your crew, and requests between accounts on the same phone

Deleting an account deletes its share of that. Deleting the app takes the lot.

Your camera and photos

The app never opens your camera or microphone — it has no code that can. The one time it touches your photos is when you pick an avatar, and that goes through Apple's own picker, which runs outside the app: it shows you your library, you choose one image, and only that image is copied in. The app can't see the rest of your photos, and it never asks for permission to, because it doesn't need it.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage statistics
  • No advertising identifier, no ad networks, no third-party code of any kind
  • No location, no contacts, no email address, no phone number
  • No account on anybody's server, because there is no server

Children

SideQuestz is for people aged 13 and over. Nothing is collected from anybody, of any age, because nothing is collected at all.

If this ever changes

If anything ever syncs between phones, this page changes first, the app says so plainly, and you get asked before anything of yours is sent anywhere.

Asking about any of this

Write to sidequestzapp@gmail.com. One person reads it, and you'll get a straight answer.
