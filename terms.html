The terms

Version 2 · updated 19 October 2026

Word for word what the app shows you before you can post anything, and what you agreed to when you tapped through it. It's also in the app at any time: Settings → Help → The terms.

Who can use it

SideQuestz is for people aged 13 and over. Under 18, use it with a parent or guardian who knows you're on it. One person, one account — a handle isn't for pretending to be somebody else.

Quests are suggestions, not instructions

The app deals you an idea. Whether it's safe, legal and sensible where you are is your call, every time, and the Quest Code sits above all of it. Nothing illegal, nothing dangerous, and you stop the moment it stops being fun for everyone involved — including people who never signed up for it.

The people around you

Half of these quests happen near strangers who didn't choose to be in one. Ask before you film anything, take the first no, and drop it if somebody changes their mind. Nobody's day gets worse so yours can be funnier — that's rule four of the Quest Code and it's the one that matters most out there.

Everything stays on this phone

There is no server. Accounts, crews, quests and progress are files on this device and nowhere else, which is why the people you add have to be accounts on this same phone. Nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere, by us or to us.

Names and handles

The one thing other people on this phone see is what you call yourself, so names and handles are filtered as they're typed. The filter is blunt and sometimes refuses a word that was perfectly innocent — a surname, a place. If that happens, write and it gets fixed.

No tolerance for abuse

The line, such as it is with everything on one device: nothing you put in front of somebody else on this phone can be a slur, a threat, a way of targeting a particular person, or anything sexual. Use the app to make somebody's day worse and you're done with it.

  • Slurs or hate aimed at who somebody is
  • Threats, harassment, or going after a particular person
  • Anything sexual, and anything whatsoever involving a child
  • Pretending to be somebody else

Blocking

Block anyone on this phone, any time, from their profile. It takes effect immediately: no requests and no XP from them reach you again, and they're out of your crew.

Your data, and getting rid of it

Nothing is collected, because nothing leaves the device. Deleting an account deletes its profile, its crew and its whole log, and that can't be undone. Deleting the app takes every account with it. Ask at sidequestzapp@gmail.com if you want to know what's held about you — the answer is short.

The app comes as it is

SideQuestz is made by one person and comes with no promise that it'll work perfectly or stay available, and none that a phone that breaks won't take your progress with it. What happens on a quest is on the people doing it. None of this takes away rights you have under the law where you live that can't be signed away.

If these terms change

A changed version is a new version, and you'll be asked again before you carry on. Carrying on is agreeing to it.

Reaching a person

Anything at all — a name the filter refused, a question about your data, something the app got wrong: sidequestzapp@gmail.com. One person reads it.
