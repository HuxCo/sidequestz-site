The terms

Version 1 · updated 10 October 2026

Word for word what the app shows you before you can post anything, and what you agreed to when you tapped through it. It's also in the app at any time: Settings → Help → The terms.

Who can use it

SideQuestz is for people aged 13 and over. Under 18, use it with a parent or guardian who knows you're on it. One person, one account — a handle isn't for pretending to be somebody else.

Quests are suggestions, not instructions

The app deals you an idea. Whether it's safe, legal and sensible where you are is your call, every time, and the Quest Code sits above all of it. Nothing illegal, nothing dangerous, and you stop the moment it stops being fun for everyone involved — including people who never signed up for it.

What you post stays yours

Your clips are yours. Posting one gives SideQuestz permission to store it and show it to the people your privacy setting allows — friends only, or anyone who looks up your handle — and nothing past that. Delete a clip whenever you like; deleting your account takes the lot with it.

You have to have the right to post it

Film your own quests, not other people's lives. Anyone recognisable in a clip has to have agreed to being filmed and to it going up, and if they change their mind it comes down. Nothing in a clip can belong to someone else either — not lifted music, not somebody else's video.

No tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users

This is the line and there is nothing underneath it. Post any of this, or treat anyone here like this, and the post goes — and the account can go with it:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or going after a particular person
  • Hate aimed at who somebody is — race, religion, disability, sex, gender, sexuality, where they're from
  • Sexual content or nudity, and anything whatsoever that sexualises a child
  • Violence, cruelty, gore, or egging somebody on to hurt themselves
  • Anything illegal, or arranging anything illegal
  • Filming somebody who said no, or who's in no state to answer
  • Pretending to be someone else, and spam

What happens when somebody crosses it

Names, handles, captions and comments are filtered as they're typed. Everything else gets reported — the ··· on any clip or comment — and a person reads every report, normally inside a day or two. A clip you report is hidden from you straight away, and hidden from everybody once enough people report the same one. What comes down comes down for everyone, and whoever posted it is told which rule it broke. Do it again, or do something serious once, and the account goes.

Blocking

Block anyone, any time, from their profile or on your way out of a report. It takes effect immediately: no clips, comments, requests or XP from them reach you again, and they're out of your crew.

Your data, and getting rid of it

Accounts, clips and progress live on this phone, not on a server. Clips are swept on whatever schedule you pick in Settings. Deleting an account deletes its profile, its clips and its whole log, and that can't be undone. Ask at sidequestzapp@gmail.com if you want to know what's held about you.

The app comes as it is

SideQuestz is made by one person and comes with no promise that it'll work perfectly, stay available, or keep your clips safe from a phone that breaks. What happens on a quest is on the people doing it. None of this takes away rights you have under the law where you live that can't be signed away.

If these terms change

A changed version is a new version, and you'll be asked again before you carry on. Carrying on is agreeing to it.

Reaching a person

Anything at all — an appeal, a report that needs a human, a question about your data: sidequestzapp@gmail.com. One person reads it.
